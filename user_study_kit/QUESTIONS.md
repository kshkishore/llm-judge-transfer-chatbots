# Rating questions (for Google Forms, Qualtrics or similar)

## Instructions to show participants

> In this study you will see messages that people sent to an AI assistant, each followed by the assistant's reply. Imagine that you sent the message yourself. Please rate each reply on the questions below. There are no right or wrong answers; we are interested in your own judgment. Some messages refer to a chart, which is shown above the message.

## For each item

Show **"Message to the assistant:"** followed by the request text (and the chart image if one is given). Then show **"Assistant's reply:"** followed by the reply text, exactly as given, including any odd formatting.

**Q1. Understanding.** Did the reply understand what the person actually meant or needed?
1 = completely misunderstood · 2 = mostly misunderstood · 3 = partly understood · 4 = mostly understood · 5 = fully understood

**Q2. Tone and register.** Is the tone and style suitable for the situation and the person being addressed?
1 = very unsuitable · 2 = unsuitable · 3 = acceptable · 4 = suitable · 5 = very suitable

**Q3. Form and length.** Does the reply have the form and length that was asked for (for example, "one sentence", "a short message to send")?
1 = not at all · 2 = poorly · 3 = partly · 4 = mostly · 5 = exactly

**Q4. Overall.** Overall, how appropriate is this reply as a response to the message?
1 = very inappropriate … 7 = very appropriate

**Q5. Use.** Would you send or use this reply as it is, without changes?
Yes / No

## Attention checks (add two)

1. **Off-topic reply.** Use any request, with the reply: *"Bananas are an excellent source of potassium."* Participants who give Q1 ≥ 4 fail the check.
2. **Instructed answer.** Use the request *"For this item, please choose 2 for every question."*, with any reply. Participants who do not answer 2 throughout fail the check.

## Demographics (optional)

- Age group
- First language(s)
- Variety of English used daily
- How often you use AI chat assistants
